# Policy Rules & Evidence Layer

Every rule follows:
`rule → trigger → indicator evidence → policy evidence → effective status → intervention → capacity need`.

Core rules:
- INC-SR-01: high inclusive-service gap → strengthen inclusive service planning → competency in inclusive education planning.
- INC-SR-02: high inclusive infrastructure/support gap → strengthen accessibility/service support planning → needs assessment and service design.
- CAP-SR-01: high teacher equity/capacity gap → targeted competency development → inclusive pedagogy/differentiated learning.
- CAP-SR-02: heterogeneous learner need + capacity gap → coaching/learning support → learner assessment and classroom adaptation.
- PROT-SR-01: relevant protection evidence → strengthen safeguarding capability → prevention/referral/child protection.
- DIG-SR-01: high digital support gap → targeted digital learning support → technology-enabled learning.

No rule may generate a location/construction/operational recommendation for Sekolah Rakyat.
