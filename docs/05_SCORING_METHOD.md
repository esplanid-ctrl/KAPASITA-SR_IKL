# Scoring Method & Weight Basis

The weights are not taken from an official regulation and are not estimated causal coefficients. They are transparent MVP design assumptions.

- 30% INC: primary substantive focus is inclusive service readiness.
- 25% CAP: service quality depends strongly on human-resource/capacity readiness.
- 20% SOC: socioeconomic vulnerability is central to the Sekolah Rakyat context but should not dominate.
- 15% DIG: digital support is enabling infrastructure, not a proxy for education quality.
- 10% POL: policy signal provides evidence context without allowing textual policy judgment to dominate quantitative evidence.

Bias controls:
1. rates/coverage/percentiles instead of raw counts where possible;
2. standardized region codes;
3. denominator compatibility checks;
4. missingness and confidence shown separately;
5. sensitivity analysis;
6. no causal claims from cross-sectional data.
